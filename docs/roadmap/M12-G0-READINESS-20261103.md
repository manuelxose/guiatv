## 4. Kill switch, limits, quality, audit (summary)
- **Kill**: the only runtime kill (file/env, no restart) belongs to the entity canary, whose pages are `noindex` by design. Indexable-page policies need an env change + restart, and no switch-triggered purge was found (the sitemap cache has `invalidateSitemapCache`, called only on precompute/blog saves; SSR/nginx cache behaviour was **not inspected**).
- **Limits**: server-side cap exists only for entity serving. Indexable sitemaps are unbounded and selection is rule-based, so cannot hold "exactly these N URLs".
- **Quality**: word count alone is not the gate; the missing pieces are unique intent, template similarity, per-fact source/date, valid JSON-LD, real navigation.
- **Audit**: no record of why a URL is indexable.

## 5. Search Console
Existing integration can supply real history for guides only. For any programmatic baseline an owner export (Performance CSV, `Dates`/`Pages`) or an extension of the monitor to sections is needed. **Family decision (data-sufficiency dry run, read-only GETs, no page created)**: the strongest candidate was "films scheduled per channel" (first-party, verified work links, closest to the only section with impressions, `/peliculas`). Measured on the public schedule for today + tomorrow, 68 channels (TDT + autonomic): 21 have at least one film, 15 at least two, **5 have three or more films with a verified `/peliculas` link, and only 1 reaches 300 words of synopsis text** (`be_mad`; a feed channel). M12 stays **NO-GO**; the blocker is now data and demand, not controls. Reopen when a source of distinct, dated, first-party content exists (for example accumulated broadcast history beyond the 3-day horizon, or owner-supplied editorial datasets) and the dry run is repeated against it.
